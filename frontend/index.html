How it works

Three steps from a ticker to a valuation.

We rebuilt a full three-statement model and DCF in code, using a working Excel model as the spec. Here's the path it takes.

1

Pull the financials

Enter any US-listed ticker. We fetch five years of income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements and reconcile the line items against the reported figures.

2

Set your assumptions

Adjust revenue growth, margins, and capital spending year by year. The engine projects all three statements forward — working capital, depreciation, and PP&E schedules included — and keeps them linked.

3

Run the DCF

WACC is built from live beta and treasury data — or override it yourself. You get an intrinsic value per share, the full discounting schedule, and a sensitivity grid across discount rate and terminal growth.

Pick a company to value.
