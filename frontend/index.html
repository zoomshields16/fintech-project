How It Works

From ticker to valuation
in three steps

No black box. Every assumption is yours to set, every formula is visible.

01

Enter a Ticker

Type any US-listed ticker and we pull 5 years of historical income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements in real time via the FMP API.

02

Build Your 3-Statement Model

Set revenue growth, margins, and capex assumptions. The engine projects IS, CF, and BS for 5 years — with working capital, D&A, PPE, and ratio schedules below.

03

Run the DCF

CAPM-based WACC auto-computed from live beta and treasury data. Outputs intrinsic value with a 25-scenario sensitivity matrix across WACC and terminal growth rate.

Features

Built from scratch

Every component is written from the ground up — no shortcuts, no hidden assumptions.

Live FMP Data

Financials pulled directly from Financial Modeling Prep — real data, updated daily, no estimates.

Integrated 3-Statement Model

IS, CF, and BS are fully linked — revenue changes flow through to free cash flow automatically.

CAPM WACC

Beta, 10Y treasury rate, and market risk premium fetched automatically. Override any input to run your own scenario.

25-Point Sensitivity Matrix

5×5 WACC vs. terminal growth rate table across 4 outputs: enterprise value, equity value, per-share price, and premium/discount.

Full DCF Schedules

WACC schedule, discounted CF schedule, and interest rate schedule — each step shown explicitly so you can follow the math.

Supporting Schedules

Working capital, D&A, PPE, UFCF build-up, and key ratios below the 3-statement model.
